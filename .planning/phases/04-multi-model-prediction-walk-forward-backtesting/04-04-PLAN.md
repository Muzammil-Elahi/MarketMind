<objective>
Build the two fastest, zero-Prophet-risk forecast models (SMA random-walk-with-drift baseline, and XGBoost direct-horizon quantile regression) behind one uniform `forecast_forward(..., horizon_days) -> {"forecast", "ci_lower", "ci_upper"}` interface, satisfying D-01's model set for the two non-Prophet options and PRED-02/PRED-03.

Purpose: these two models have zero import risk (no compiled-artifact dependency, unlike Prophet -- Plan 05), so they can be built, tested, and reused by Plan 06's `backtest.py`/`engine.py` independently of Prophet's own risk profile. `xgboost_model.py`'s `forecast_forward` wrapper is the one genuinely novel piece of math this plan introduces: XGBoost's `fit_predict` only produces a single horizon-endpoint prediction (not a day-by-day path, per the direct-horizon strategy), so `forecast_forward` interpolates that endpoint into a path shape matching SMA's and Prophet's own outputs -- this is what lets `engine.py` (Plan 06) dispatch to any of the three models through one identical call signature.

Output: `src/prediction/sma_model.py`, `src/prediction/xgboost_model.py`, plus two test files -- all green, zero network calls, XGBoost fixtures kept small (<=750 rows) to bound training time.
</objective>
